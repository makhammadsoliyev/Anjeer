# 8. PHASE 5 — RAG (weeks 22–26)

The most valuable phase of the course. The keyword search built in week 8 is upgraded step by step into a production RAG system — and quality is measured at every step.

> **This is where the interview story is born**
>
> "I built RAG" — everyone says that. "I started with keyword search, found 11 of 20 real queries; changed the chunking strategy, added hybrid search and re-ranking, pushed Recall@5 to 0.87" — very few people can say that.
> That's why the week-8 baseline and the week-11 search log mattered so much.
